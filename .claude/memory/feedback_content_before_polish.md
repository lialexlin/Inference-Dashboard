---
name: Content before polish
description: Alex iterates on substance and structure first, only builds visual/code after content is locked down
type: feedback
---

When building presentations, documents, or any deliverable with both content and visual components, Alex wants to iterate on the substance first. Don't jump to building the HTML/code/design until the content structure is agreed upon.

**Why:** In the AI at Axiom presentation (2026-03-29), Alex said "let's not build yet, let's continue to discuss the structure" and later "ok actually let's focus on the content first" after the HTML was already being refined. Multiple rounds of visual polish were done before the content was settled, creating unnecessary rework.

**How to apply:** When a task involves both content and presentation, propose and iterate on the content outline in markdown first. Only move to implementation (HTML, slides, formatting) once Alex signals the structure and substance are solid. Content > design > animation, in that order.
